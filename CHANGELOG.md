## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added

- Initial public release of `teslasuit_rapidkit`, MIT licensed.
- `ClosedLoopEngine`, `ControlStrategyBase`, `MuscleMap`, `CalibrationAPI`,
  `SharedRingBuffer`, and LabStreamingLayer (LSL) integration.
- Six example applications: `atomic`, `generic_gui`, `elbow_flexion`,
  `haptic_navigation`, `haptic_proximity_radar`, `vestibular_training`.
